# Medical and nutrition disclaimer

**Last updated:** 2026-10-01

## 1. What the app is for

NutriRoutine helps you follow the diet and workout plan given to you by a licensed professional (doctor, dietitian, nutritionist; for training, a qualified professional). The app does not create diets, does not calculate requirements and gives no nutritional, medical or sports advice: it records what you enter, organizes it and reminds you of it.

## 2. It does not replace your doctor or nutritionist

The information shown is for organizational and informational purposes only. It is not a diagnosis, prescription, therapy or personalized advice and in no way replaces your doctor or the professional who follows you. The app is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any health condition. Using it creates no doctor-patient or professional-client relationship with the developer. For any doubt about your diet or health, ask your professional.

## 3. Indicative nutrition values

Calories and macronutrients are estimates calculated from public databases (listed in Sources) based on average food values: they may differ from actual values. Values for scanned products come from the label as declared by the producer and entered by volunteers (Open Food Facts) or by you: they remain indicative. They are not targets and must not be used for clinical decisions, for example dosing medication or insulin.

## 4. Who it is for

The app is meant for healthy adults following a plan prescribed by a professional. With medical conditions (for example diabetes, cardiovascular, kidney or gastrointestinal disease), eating disorders, food allergies or intolerances, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, and for anyone under 18, do not rely on the app: follow only the healthcare professionals who look after you and use it, if they agree, only as a reminder.

## 5. Workouts

Only do exercises set by a professional and suited to your condition. Before starting or increasing physical activity, check your fitness with a doctor. If during training or the diet you feel pain, discomfort or unusual symptoms, stop and contact a doctor; in an emergency call 112.

## 6. Sample diet

The sample diet only shows how the app works. Meals, quantities, times and values are for demonstration, were not designed for you and must not be followed. After trying it, delete it and enter the diet you received from your professional.

You use the app under your full and sole responsibility. You are responsible for the accuracy of the data you enter and for checking it with your professional. To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for damage, injury, side effects or health problems arising from use of the app other than as described, from incorrectly entered data or from following directions not prescribed by a professional. Consumer rights that cannot be excluded by law are not affected.
