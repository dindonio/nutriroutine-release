# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-01

## In short

Your data stays on your device and, with iCloud sync (on by default, you can turn it off in You, Privacy and data), in your private iCloud. It is not sent to the developer's servers or to other third parties. The app connects only to Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode that isn't saved yet it sends that code to read its values and, only if you choose to share a new product, also that product's data and photos, published with the NutriRoutine account and without your name. You don't need to create any account, and the app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data controller

Antonio Scognamiglio, private individual, developer of NutriRoutine. Contact: github.com/dindonio/nutriroutine-release (Issues section).

## What data the app uses

The profile you enter (your name, optional, and starting weight); health-related data: the diet, logged meals, weigh-ins and check-ups, glasses of water, routines and workouts; the pantry status and shopping list; the products you scan or enter (brand and label values); your nutritionist's contact details if you enter them. Health-related data is "special category data" under Article 9 of the GDPR.

## Why and on what basis

Only to make the app work: showing your plan, reminding you of meals, water and check-ups, calculating totals and charts. No profiling, advertising or sale of data. The legal basis is your explicit consent to the processing of health data (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)) and the use of the service you chose (Article 6(1)(b)).

## Where it is stored

Locally on your device, in the app's and its widgets' container, protected by iOS encryption tied to your passcode. If iCloud sync is on (You, Privacy and data), a copy is kept in your iCloud account's private database, managed by Apple under the iCloud terms you accepted: only you access it with your account. With iCloud Advanced Data Protection the copy is end-to-end encrypted. Notifications and Live Activities are generated by the device itself.

## External connections

The app has no servers of its own. When you scan a barcode that isn't saved yet, it sends only that barcode to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to read its values: the request contains none of the data you enter in the app, apart from what any Internet connection involves, such as the device's IP address, which Open Food Facts handles under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share a new product, its name, brand, quantity, values and the photos you take for the occasion are published on Open Food Facts with the NutriRoutine account, without your name, under their licenses (ODbL for data, CC BY-SA for photos): they become public and anyone can reuse them. Photos are sent without location data. Even then you are not sending your profile's or your diet's data, only the product's. The camera is used only to read the barcode and take those photos: the images are not saved in the app. The nutrition database is included in the app and works offline. Files you export (a diet or a backup) go only where you decide.

## How long

As long as you keep it in the app. "Delete all data" in You, Privacy and data deletes it from the device and, with sync on, from iCloud and your other devices. Uninstalling the app deletes local data; the iCloud copy can also be managed from your account's iCloud settings.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability (GDPR articles 15 to 22). Since the data is only on your devices and in your iCloud, you exercise these rights directly in the app: you view and correct it in each section, export it with the full backup in a readable file (JSON), and delete it with "Delete all data". You can withdraw consent at any time: without consent the app cannot process the data, so withdrawal means deleting it; withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).

## Minors

The app is meant for adults. Under 14, consent must be given by whoever holds parental responsibility.

## Changes

If this policy or the disclaimer change substantially, the app shows you and asks for consent again before continuing.
